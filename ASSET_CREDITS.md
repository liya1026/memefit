## Characters and visual assets

The selectable characters reference Marge and Homer from *The Simpsons*.

theme. These recognisable references are not presented as wholly
original characters. This is an unofficial student project, with no

are not bundled; neither are remote images or fonts. The poster is a
promotional composition, not an unedited screenshot of gameplay.
